Methodology: Momentum uses percentile ranks within each channel and platform cohort. Publishing windows blend unique commenters, comment intent weights, and snapshot-to-snapshot view/like/share/comment growth in IST. Each signal is scaled to its own peak before weighting so platforms and metrics stay comparable. Gaps longer than four hours between snapshots are ignored. Audience intent uses local deterministic rules and does not send comment text to an external AI service. Suggestions are hypotheses supported by associations; an observed outcome does not prove causation.
