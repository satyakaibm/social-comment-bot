Methodology: The Instagram Most active times card is not modelled: it is Meta's own online_followers insight (the data behind the Instagram app's chart), averaged per hour over the days held and shown on the IST clock. Momentum uses percentile ranks within each channel and platform cohort. Engagement windows blend unique commenters, comment intent weights, and snapshot-to-snapshot like/share/view growth in IST; comment-count growth is excluded because it includes the bot's own replies. They describe when your existing audience is active, not when to publish. Each signal is scaled to its own peak before weighting so platforms and metrics stay comparable. Gaps longer than four hours between snapshots are ignored. Audience intent uses local deterministic rules and does not send comment text to an external AI service. Suggestions are hypotheses supported by associations; an observed outcome does not prove causation.
