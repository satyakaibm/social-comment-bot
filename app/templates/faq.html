FAQ

Definitions for the numbers and statuses shown on the dashboard. The top counts on the dashboard reflect the time range you pick there; the statuses below reflect comments currently saved in the table.

How to read these numbers

Comments found
New comments the bot saw during the selected time range.
Bot replies sent
Comments the bot answered during the selected time range.
Had a reply already
Comments your account had answered before the bot checked.
Comments taken care of
Bot replies sent plus comments that already had a reply.
Pending review
A reply is drafted and waiting for the automation to process it.
Posting
The reply is currently claimed by an active publishing process.
Posted
The automation successfully published the reply.
Failed
The platform rejected the reply or returned an error; it remains available for retry.
Already replied
An existing reply from your account was found, so the automation avoided a duplicate.
Rejected
The comment was intentionally excluded, such as when it exceeded the configured age limit, or Meta rejected the reply on every attempt (see META_MAX_POST_ATTEMPTS) and it will not be retried automatically.
Why do "Bot replies sent" and "Sent replies still saved" sometimes differ?
The bot remembers the total number sent. Old comment details may be removed later to save space, so the saved-record count can be lower than the all-time total.
