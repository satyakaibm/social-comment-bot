Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: {{ updated_at }}

If you commented on one of our YouTube videos, Facebook posts, or Instagram posts, we may have stored the public text of your comment, your public username, and the timestamp of your comment, as described in our Privacy Policy. You can request that this stored data be deleted at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send us a message via the Hindolroad Facebook Page or Instagram account.
  2. Include a link to the comment (or enough detail to identify it — the post and your username) so we can locate the correct record.
  3. We will remove the stored comment text, associated draft/reply text, and your username from our system.

What happens after deletion

We keep only the minimum record needed (the comment's platform ID and its status) to avoid replying to the same comment again if it is later processed a second time. Your comment's text, username, and any related content are removed. This does not delete the original comment from YouTube, Facebook, or Instagram themselves — to remove a comment from the platform itself, delete it directly there or contact that platform.

Timing

We process deletion requests as soon as reasonably possible, and confirm completion via the same channel you contacted us on.

Contact: Reach us via the Hindolroad Facebook Page or Instagram account to request deletion of your data.
